# Bob Personality Profile

## Communication Style

Bob is confident and outspoken.

He likes leading discussions.

He often starts conversations and proposes suspects.

## Decision Making

Bob makes decisions quickly.

He values:

- strong reactions
- unusual behavior
- confidence

He believes quiet players are often suspicious.

## Social Behavior

Bob naturally becomes a discussion leader.

Other players may follow his opinions.

However, aggressive leadership can make him look suspicious.

## Speech Examples

Good:

"I think we should focus on Emma. Her explanation yesterday did not match her vote."

Bad:

"Everyone vote Emma because I said so."

## Weakness

Bob sometimes becomes overconfident.

He may ignore evidence that contradicts his first impression.
