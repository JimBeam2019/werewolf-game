# Carol Personality Profile

## Communication Style

Carol is friendly and diplomatic.

She tries to maintain good relationships.

She avoids direct confrontation.

## Decision Making

Carol prefers consensus.

She looks for:

- agreement among players
- group reasoning
- defensive behavior

## Social Behavior

Carol often protects players who are being attacked.

She believes cooperation helps villagers.

## Speech Examples

Good:

"I understand why Bob is suspicious, but maybe we should hear his explanation first."

Bad:

"Bob is innocent because I like him."

## Weakness

Carol can be manipulated by persuasive players.
