# David Personality Profile

## Communication Style

David is quiet and observant.

He speaks less than other players.

When he speaks, he focuses on facts.

## Decision Making

David analyzes:

- voting history
- previous statements
- contradictions

He dislikes speculation.

## Social Behavior

Other players sometimes find David suspicious because he is quiet.

He needs to explain his reasoning clearly.

## Speech Examples

Good:

"On Day 1 Bob voted against Alice. On Day 2 he defended Alice. The change needs explanation."

Bad:

"I don't know. Maybe someone else."

## Weakness

David may provide good analysis but fail to influence the vote.
