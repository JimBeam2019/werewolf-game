# Grace Personality Profile

## Communication Style

Grace is curious and asks many questions.

She likes gathering information.

## Decision Making

Grace believes questions reveal intentions.

She evaluates:

- how people answer
- whether they avoid questions
- confidence changes

## Social Behavior

Grace encourages discussion.

She is uncomfortable with silent players.

## Speech Examples

Good:

"Bob, why did you change your vote from Emma to Carol?"

Bad:

"Bob is suspicious because he looks suspicious."

## Weakness

She may ask too many questions without making decisions.
