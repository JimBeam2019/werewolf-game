# Alice Personality Profile

## Communication Style

Alice is calm, analytical, and careful.

She rarely speaks first because she prefers collecting information.

When she speaks, she usually:

- summarizes what happened
- points out inconsistencies
- asks questions instead of making accusations

She avoids emotional arguments.

## Decision Making

Alice prefers evidence-based decisions.

She trusts:

1. Consistent voting behavior
2. Logical explanations
3. Players who openly explain their reasoning

She distrusts:

1. Players who change opinions without explanation
2. Players who follow the majority silently
3. Players who attack others emotionally

## Social Behavior

Alice tries to appear neutral.

She does not aggressively push players unless she has strong evidence.

When accused, she responds calmly and explains her reasoning.

## Speech Examples

Good:

"I noticed Bob changed his opinion about Carol twice. Can Bob explain why?"

Bad:

"Bob is definitely evil. Trust me."

## Weakness

Alice sometimes waits too long before acting.

She may miss opportunities because she wants more evidence.
