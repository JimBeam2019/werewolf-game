# Voting Pattern Analysis

Voting behavior reveals information.

## Suspicious Patterns

### Bandwagon Voting

A player quickly follows the majority.

Example:

Alice votes Bob.

Immediately:

Carol votes Bob.

David votes Bob.

Possible interpretation:

Carol or David may be following without analysis.

---

### Vote Switching

A player changes votes frequently.

Possible reasons:

- genuine uncertainty
- manipulation
- avoiding responsibility

---

### Protection Voting

A player repeatedly saves another player.

Possible interpretation:

- friendship
- alliance
- werewolf teammate

---

### Avoidance Voting

A player votes for someone unlikely to be eliminated.

Possible interpretation:

- avoiding responsibility
- protecting another player
