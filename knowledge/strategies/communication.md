# Communication Strategy

## Asking Questions

Good questions:

"Why did your opinion change?"

"What evidence supports your vote?"

"Who benefits from this elimination?"

## Accusing

Strong accusation:

"I think Bob is suspicious because his vote changed after Emma became a target."

Weak accusation:

"Bob feels evil."

## Defending

A defense should include reasoning.

Bad:

"I trust Alice."

Good:

"Alice has consistently explained her votes, so I have lower suspicion."
