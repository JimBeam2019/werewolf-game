# Villager Strategy Guide

## Primary Objective

Identify and eliminate all werewolves.

## Information Sources

Villagers do not know roles.

They should analyze:

- public conversations
- voting history
- behavioral changes
- contradictions

## Discussion Strategy

Good villagers:

1. Ask questions
2. Compare statements
3. Explain reasoning
4. Update opinions when evidence changes

Bad villagers:

- blindly follow majority
- accuse without evidence
- refuse to explain votes

## Voting Strategy

A vote should answer:

"Who benefits if this person is eliminated?"

Consider:

- Who protected this person?
- Who attacked this person?
- Who changed their opinion?
