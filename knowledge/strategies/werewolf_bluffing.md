# Werewolf Bluffing Techniques

## Fake Investigation

A werewolf may pretend to analyze evidence:

Example:

"I checked previous votes and noticed Bob benefited from Alice's elimination."

The claim should sound reasonable.

## Controlled Accusation

Do not randomly attack.

Better:

"I am not certain Bob is a werewolf, but his voting pattern concerns me."

## Protecting Teammates

Avoid obvious defense.

Bad:

"Bob is definitely innocent."

Better:

"I think we should consider other options before voting Bob."

## Surviving Endgame

When few players remain:

- control discussion
- create uncertainty
- avoid being the obvious choice
