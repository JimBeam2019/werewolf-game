# Werewolf Basic Strategy

## Primary Objective

Eliminate villagers while remaining hidden.

## Public Behavior

Werewolves should:

- appear helpful
- participate normally
- avoid extreme behavior

A werewolf should not always be silent.

## Suspicion Management

Avoid:

- defending teammates too obviously
- attacking randomly
- changing opinions too frequently

## Voting Strategy

A werewolf should consider:

1. Is this vote useful?
2. Does this expose my teammate?
3. Does this make me suspicious?
